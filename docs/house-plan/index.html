4. How the router uses this plan

There is no fixed lap any more. Costa's brief: clump work, never carry something to a room and walk straight back, and be free to throw in a quick trip when a room nearby has one small thing. So the app now carries this plan as a graph of doors and hallway points with rough distances in metres, and builds each lap from what's actually on the list:

  1. Anything that runs unattended (a wash, the dishwasher) goes first. Anything with a deadline goes next.
  2. From wherever you are, go to the nearest room with something ready to do, and do everything in it. A room with several jobs pulls slightly harder than a room with one.
  3. If a job you just did carries something to another room, go there next and do that room while you're in it.
  4. If a job in the room you're in is waiting on something elsewhere (fold on the couch, washing still in the laundry), go and do that first, then come back.
  5. A job that needs another done first waits until it is. You never walk back for a prerequisite.

The two figures below were drawn before that change and are kept as the worked example: the fixed order the app used to follow, and the single loop the plan suggested. The router will produce the loop on the right when the list is spread evenly through the house, and something shorter when it isn't.
